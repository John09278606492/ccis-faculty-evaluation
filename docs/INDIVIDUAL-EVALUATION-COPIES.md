# Submitted Annex A and B copies

Open **Reports → Individual Evaluation Copies**. Only submitted Annex A/B responses appear; no blank copies are generated for students who have not evaluated.

- **Print Copy** opens one response inside a modal.
- Tick rows and use **Print Selected Copies** for a chosen batch.
- Filter by instrument or assignment, then use **Print All Filtered Copies** for every matching response, including other table pages.
- Annex A prints **two distinct submitted responses side by side, lengthwise**, per **8.5 × 13-inch portrait** sheet, matching the original two-column form. A single/odd selection leaves the unused half blank; responses are never silently duplicated.
- Annex B follows its original format: one supervisor response continues through two columns on a sheet. Longer customized content/comments may continue onto another sheet; nothing is clipped.
- Use the Print / Save PDF button inside the preview. Choose **8.5 × 13 inches (long bond/Folio), Portrait, 100% scale**, and disable browser headers/footers. The preview itself uses the same paper dimensions and 0.5-inch margins.

Copies reproduce the submitted instrument snapshot, faculty/course information, selected ratings, section descriptions, comments, total score and percentage. They do not fabricate handwritten signatures. Missing ratings are marked incomplete. Published edits cannot rewrite criteria on an existing submitted copy.

Identities are hidden by default as Student 1, Student 2 or Supervisor. Hidden-mode HTML does not contain evaluator names or student IDs. **Show Identity** requires **View Evaluator Identity** (`view_evaluator_identity_form`) under Evaluation Forms in Roles & Permissions. Existing super-admin roles receive it; other roles must be explicitly granted it. Staff also need report/form viewing permission and access to the response campus. Preview selections expire after thirty minutes and belong to the staff account that created them.

New submissions keep a separate encrypted evaluator-identity snapshot, excluded from ordinary model serialization. Earlier authenticated submissions can be resolved from their existing user reference or keyed submission fingerprint when the matching campus account is still available. Truly anonymous or unresolvable records display Identity unavailable; identities are never guessed. Changing Hide/Show controls the printed copy and does not change answers or ratings.
